Ami Sampler for macOS 10.9 Mavericks and newer (Intel Macs)
=================================================================

1. Copy "Ami Sampler.vst3" to:       /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
2. Copy "Ami Sampler.component" to:  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   (the AU version is for Logic, GarageBand and other AU hosts)
3. "Ami Sampler.app" is the standalone version; put it in /Applications.

The plugin is not signed. If macOS refuses to open it, run this in Terminal:

   sudo xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Ami Sampler.vst3"
   sudo xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Ami Sampler.component"

For the standalone app: right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.

Restart your DAW and rescan plugins. For AU in Logic, you may need to log
out and back in once so macOS picks up the new component.

Note: the host must support VST3 or AU. Ableton Live 9 only supports VST2
and AU, so use the AU version there.
